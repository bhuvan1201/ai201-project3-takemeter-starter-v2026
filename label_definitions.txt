question: Mainly asks readers for information, an explanation, advice, or their views, rather than presenting the author’s own answer.
proposal: Mainly recommends a specific action or change that the author wants someone to make.
experience: Mainly recounts a specific event the author personally experienced as an F1 fan, including their response to that event.
opinion: Mainly presents the author’s judgment, preference, or interpretation, rather than requesting an answer, recommending a specific change, or recounting a personal event.
